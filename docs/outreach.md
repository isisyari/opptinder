## 1. Pathways to Science (Institute for Broadening Participation) — permission to reuse listings

**To:** the contact on https://www.pathwaystoscience.org/Contactus.aspx
**Subject:** Permission to link to Pathways to Science program listings in a student project

Hello,

I'm [Your name], an international student at [University] on an F-1 visa. I'm building a small,
free tool that helps other F-1 students find resume-building opportunities they're actually
eligible for: research programs, hackathons, conferences and fellowships that don't require
U.S. citizenship or off-campus work authorization.

Pathways to Science is the only database I've found that lets students filter research programs
by "open to non-U.S. citizens," which is exactly the gap my project addresses. I'd like to
include your undergraduate, non-U.S.-citizen program listings in the tool.

Specifically, I'd:

- Fetch the public search-results page for undergraduate + non-U.S.-citizen programs
  about once a week (one request, the same page a visitor sees).
- Show only the program name, host institution, location, a one-line summary and a link that
  sends students to the program's page on pathwaystoscience.org for deadlines and details.
- Credit "Pathways to Science / IBP" on every card and link back to your site.

I would not copy full program descriptions, deadlines or contact details, and I'm happy to
adjust anything (frequency, fields shown, attribution wording) to fit your preferences. If
you have a data feed or partner program you'd rather I use, I'd gladly do that instead.

Would that be all right with you?

Thank you for the work you do,
[Your name]
[University, program, year]
[Email]

## 2. IEFA.org — partnership / data-feed request

**To:** IEFA contact form or the address on https://www.iefa.org/contact
**Subject:** Student project: could we link to IEFA scholarship listings?

Hello,

I'm [Your name], an international student at [University] on an F-1 visa, building a free tool
that helps international students in the U.S. find opportunities they're eligible for without
work authorization: scholarships, research programs, hackathons and conferences.

IEFA's scholarship database stands out because it records nationality eligibility and host
country for each award, which is exactly what international students need and almost no other
source provides. I've read your terms of use and understand the content is for personal,
non-commercial use, so I'm writing to ask rather than scrape.

Would IEFA be open to any of the following?

1. A partnership where my tool shows award names, field of study, nationality eligibility and
   host country, with every card linking to the award page on iefa.org (no amounts, deadlines
   or details copied), with clear IEFA attribution.
2. A data feed or API, if one exists for partners.
3. Any other arrangement you'd prefer.

The project is non-commercial and student-run. I'm glad to share what I've built, add any
attribution or tracking links you need, or cap how often we refresh.

Thank you for considering it,
[Your name]
[University, program, year]
[Email]
